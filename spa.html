Spa & Wellness at Merlune

Welcome to a sanctuary of absolute tranquility. At Merlune, we believe that true wellness is a harmony between the body, the mind, and the natural rhythms of the ocean. Our holistic spa is designed to be a completely serene escape — a place to disconnect from the world and reconnect with yourself.

Our expert therapists are trained in the restorative arts of deep-tissue healing, breathwork, and the soothing properties of natural Andaman botanicals. Every ritual is carefully crafted to bring you profound relaxation and renewed vitality during your stay.
