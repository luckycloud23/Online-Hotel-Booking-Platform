Our Story

Born of Sea & Moon

Merlune was dreamed into existence on a moonlit beach in the Andaman Islands — where bioluminescent waves whisper against ancient coral shores, and the night sky is closer than anywhere else on earth.

Named for the union of mer (sea) and lune (moon), our sanctuary is a place where time dissolves into tides and starlight. Every villa, every meal, every moment is designed to immerse you in the quiet majesty of the night.

This is not merely a resort. It is a lunar passage — an invitation to slow down, look up, and rediscover the elemental beauty that the modern world has forgotten.

Location

The Edge of the World

Merlune is located on a private island in the Andaman archipelago, India — 1,200 kilometres from the mainland, surrounded by pristine coral reefs and ancient rainforest. Accessible by seaplane from Port Blair (45 min) or private speedboat transfer.

✈️ Nearest Airport

Veer Savarkar International Airport, Port Blair (IXZ)

🛥️ Resort Transfer

45 min by seaplane or 90 min by private speedboat

🌡️ Climate

Tropical — 25°C to 32°C year round. Best: Oct–May
